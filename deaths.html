Understanding Road Trauma

Road fatalities are more than just statistics; they represent a significant social and economic cost to the community. This dashboard visualizes BITRE data to track long-term national trends, identify high-risk road user types (such as pedestrians or motorcyclists), and analyze the age demographics most affected by fatal crashes. Use the brush tool on the line chart to filter specific historical periods.
