Visualizing Road Safety Enforcement Across Australia

Explore fines, arrests, breath tests, and drug test results to understand trends and patterns across jurisdictions.

About This Project

This dashboard visualizes Australian road safety enforcement data, providing insights into trends over the years. Users can compare jurisdictions, analyze age groups, and explore different metrics like fines, arrests, and positive test rates.

The aim is to make enforcement data more accessible and to support data-driven discussions on road safety.
