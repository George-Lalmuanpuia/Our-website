Impaired Driving Enforcement

High-visibility testing is a proven deterrent against alcohol and drug-impaired driving. This dashboard analyzes the scale of Random Breath Testing (RBT) and Roadside Drug Testing (RDT) programs. Crucially, the "Test Rate" chart normalizes this data against the number of licensed drivers in each state, providing a fair comparison of enforcement intensity regardless of the state's population size.
